Security Awareness: Safe Digital Banking

At Bharat Nidhi Bank, safeguarding your hard-earned funds is our highest priority. Follow these four golden safety principles:

Never Share OTP

No bank executive will ever ask for your 6-digit One Time Password.

Protect Passwords

Never disclose net-banking passwords or write them in public spaces.

Keep PIN Secret

Cover ATM keypads while entering your 4-digit Card PIN.

Beware Phishing Links

Do not click unverified SMS links promising lottery or loan waivers.
